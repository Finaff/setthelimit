### Where Musk is uncertain or inconsistent

The gap between word and deed is the whole dossier. He rates AI "higher risk than nuclear weapons", thinks humans are unlikely to be in charge in ten years, signed a pause letter and answered Amodei's slow-down essay with "Dario is right" — and in the same summer said he would not press a stop button, that nobody can stop the momentum, and "let's enjoy the ride", while running one of the fastest build-outs in the industry. The predictions score what he would *answer*: danger items rest on verified statements (d1, d7 high). On the speed axis his words pull both ways, so s8 and s13 sit near the middle with the conflict stated in the basis; s5 leans on his conduct because he has made no clean "we must race China" statement. On regulation he moved from "referee" and SB 1047 (2023–24) to "a one-way ratchet" and peer review among rivals (2026), while his companies sue states over AI laws; s6 reflects the later view. s4, s11 and p4 are low-confidence: no statement on an inspected treaty, no stated ceiling for open weights (he releases only last-generation models), and no ranking of concentrated power against rogue AI. He gave no p(doom) number in 2026.

### Where Zuckerberg is uncertain or inconsistent

His danger views have moved more than his tone suggests. In 2017 doomsday talk was "pretty irresponsible" and in 2024 a runaway was not "particularly likely"; by August 2026 he writes that a self-improving AI "is by definition directing and advancing its own goals" and that AI holding power "puts humanity in peril" — and then answers with a balance of power rather than a brake. So d1, d3 and d5 are low but not at the floor, all `med`. On openness he went from "the path forward" (2024) to "careful about what we choose to open source" (2025) to "some open source models" (2026), with the top Muse models closed; s11 scores the legal-freedom question he would still answer yes to, not Meta's practice. On speed he rejects any imposed or coordinated slowing (s1, s5, s6 high, from the essay's own wording) yet says Meta delayed Muse for months and pledges most compute away from recursive self-improvement, which is a deliberate, self-chosen limit; s13 is therefore 72, not 95. No statement on an inspected treaty (s4 low), no lives-saved argument (s10 low), and no 20-year labour forecast — he predicts an "abundance of jobs" while Meta cuts staff (p2 low).
